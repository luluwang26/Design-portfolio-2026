“How it works” in Guided Onboarding

New customers signed up believing the platform could help, but many never saw enough value to stay. A short, skippable walkthrough teaches the product before asking anyone to configure it — turning onboarding from a checklist into a path.

Receiver First-Time-Use Redesign

New receivers came to the platform to get paid, but the first-time experience answered everything except how and when. Rebuilding it around "where's my money?", payment status first, education phased, turned confusion into transactions.

+30% invoices created +22% initial TPV

In-Product Sales Connect

A softening net-new funnel needed a fast lever. Building "talk to a human" into onboarding — with an honest "matching in progress" wait designed around a seven-minute routing latency — turned a cold-start moment into a repeatable booking funnel.

~5.5% booking rate ~25% trial conversion
